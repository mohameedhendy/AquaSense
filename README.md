# AquaSense

AquaSense is a graduation project focused on smart water management and water conservation.

The platform combines a Flutter mobile application with REST APIs, machine-learning services, and IoT-based monitoring to help users analyze water quality, detect possible leaks, monitor tank levels, and improve water consumption awareness.

## Project Overview

Water scarcity and inefficient water usage are major global challenges. AquaSense was designed to provide users with practical tools for monitoring and managing water resources through a single mobile application.

The project integrates mobile development, artificial intelligence, IoT devices, and external APIs to deliver water-related insights and monitoring features.

## Main Features

- Water potability prediction based on entered water measurements.
- Water-quality analysis and result presentation.
- Water leak detection support using machine-learning models.
- Water tank level monitoring using ultrasonic sensors.
- Water consumption tracking and usage insights.
- User-contributed map of available free water sources.
- Water conservation tips and educational content.
- News and expert guidance related to water management.
- Integration with REST APIs, machine-learning services, and IoT components.
